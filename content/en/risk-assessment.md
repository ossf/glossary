The process of identifying the potential or actual Risks introduced by a system.

Source: [Gemara](https://gemara.openssf.org/model/02-definitions)

### Industry Cross-Reference

Risk assessment is the process of identifying, estimating, and prioritizing risks to organizational operations (including mission, functions, image, reputation), assets, and individuals resulting from the operation of an information system.

Source: [NIST CSRC Glossary - Risk Assessment](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/risk_assessment)
