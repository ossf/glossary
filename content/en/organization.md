Any logical grouping of human, physical, virtual, and information resources such as a company, business unit, or team.

Source: [Gemara](https://gemara.openssf.org/model/02-definitions)

### Industry Cross-Reference

An organization is an entity of any size, complexity, or positioning within an organizational structure (e.g., a federal agency, a private company, or any of its operational elements) that defines and operates its own governance structure.

Source: [NIST CSRC Glossary - Organization](https://csrc.nist.gov/glossary/term/organization)
