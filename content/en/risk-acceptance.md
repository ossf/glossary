A clearly documented decision to accept an unmitigated Risk as necessary or unavoidable.

Source: [Gemara](https://gemara.openssf.org/model/02-definitions)

### Industry Cross-Reference

Risk acceptance is a formal, documented decision to accept the identified risk and monitor it periodically, usually because the cost of mitigation outweighs the potential loss or the risk falls within accepted thresholds.

Source: [ISACA Glossary - Risk Acceptance](https://www.isaca.org/resources/glossary)
